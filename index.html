Venezuela

Venezuela is a country blessed with spectacular geography that harbors some of the planet's greatest natural treasures, combining the grandeur of Angel Falls—the world's highest waterfall, located in the heart of the ancient Guiana Shield—with the paradisiacal landscapes of white-sand beaches and crystal-clear waters in the Los Roques Archipelago. This land of contrasts spans everything from the snow-capped peaks of the Andes Mountains to the untamed plains teeming with wildlife, all set against a backdrop of astonishing biodiversity. Yet, its greatest asset is its people, globally renowned for their warmth, resilience, infectious joy, and deep sense of hospitality—virtues reflected in a rich cultural identity, vibrant festivities, and a delicious, iconic cuisine led by the signature arepa.
